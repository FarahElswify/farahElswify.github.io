IDO Internal Platform

A redesign of the internal web platform used by IDO Logistics teams in Lisbon for store operations, logistics and internal communication. Explore the screens below and click inside them as if they were a prototype.

Explore the platform
Client
IDO Logistics, Lisbon
Role
UI/UX design intern, AIESEC
Platform
Desktop web app
Modules
6 modules, 18 screens

What I worked on

  • Reviewed the existing screens and information structure, then refined layouts, visual hierarchy and navigation.
  • Kept the original data, metrics and functionality intact, so the redesign changed how things look and flow, not what the system does.
  • Applied the existing Design System and reusable components to build consistent, clean and responsive pages across the dashboard.
