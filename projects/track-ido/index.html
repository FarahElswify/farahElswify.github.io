Track IDO

A mobile app that lets logistics staff scan an order, confirm its collection and follow each delivery from the warehouse to the customer.

Explore the screens
Client
IDO Logistics, Lisbon
Role
UI/UX designer, AIESEC internship
Platform
iOS mobile app
Scope
4 flows, 17 screens and states

The brief

IDO Logistics needed a mobile companion to its internal platform. Warehouse and delivery staff work on the move, often with one hand free, and need to confirm an order in seconds.

I designed the app’s core flows: signing in, reading an order’s QR code, checking services, and following deliveries. Every screen includes its loading, error and success states, so the developers receive a complete picture.

Explore the flows

Pick a flow, scroll sideways through its states, and tap any screen to enlarge it.

Staff sign in with their company credentials. The form moves through five states, and the main button changes with each one: it disables after success and becomes “Try Again” after an error.

Scanning is the main task, so it sits in the first tab. When a code is damaged or the camera is unavailable, “Manual entry” lets staff type the reference instead. Results appear in a focused pop-up that says exactly what happened.

Each service is one compact row: date, time, and the route from origin to destination. Skeleton rows hold the layout while data loads, so the screen doesn’t jump.

Deliveries are listed by reference with a status icon. Tapping a row opens a pop-up with the collection and delivery details, a status pill, and a button to copy the reference.

Every state is designed

Default, loading, success and error all exist for each flow. Developers don’t need to guess what happens when something goes wrong.

A fallback for every action

If scanning fails, manual entry is one tap away. If details fail to load, “Try again” is in the message itself.

Colour carries status

The same palette means the same thing on every screen, so staff can read an order’s state at a glance.
